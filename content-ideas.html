00 · Your lane

Why someone follows you

People follow a person for a reason they can say in one line. Yours: "she runs socials for brands, makes UGC, works four jobs, and shows you how she keeps it all together (and still fills her cup)." Every post should fit one of these four pillars.

35%

Busy girl life

Day-in-the-life of a full-timer with three side hustles. Timestamps, chaos, dry humour. This is your most relatable and most sendable pillar.

25%

Social media, decoded

What you know from running brand accounts: trends you're using this week, hooks that work, before/after grids. People save these.

15%

UGC creator, behind the scenes

How a brief becomes a Reel, how gifted deals work, your setup. There's a big audience of aspiring creators who want this.

25%

Fill-my-cup moments

The soft side of the busy life: solo café dates, pilates, skincare nights, Sydney summer, hotels and fit checks from your shoots. This is what makes people stay.

Reference post

Why "how I fill my cup" works

Your reference: a 10-slide carousel by @estherrvictory, "how I fill my cup as a busy girl, juggling a 9-5, my masters degree, and content creation." It has 53 reposts, more than its 32 comments. That means people shared it to their own profiles, which is exactly the reach we want. Here's what to borrow without copying.

The angle

Busy, but soft

It doesn't sell the hustle; it sells relief from it. The hook names three jobs in one breath, so anyone juggling things feels seen. Then every slide is a calm moment.

For you"How I fill my cup working 4 jobs." Your story is a more extreme version of hers, and that's the hook.
The layout

Two photos per slide

Each 4:5 slide stacks two landscape photos: a scene on top, a detail or selfie below. It fits twice the content and makes plain phone photos look edited.

For youBuild one template in Canva or Edits and reuse it. That makes the series easy to recognise and quick to make.
The text

★ headline + one soft line

Each slide gets a big bold headline with a star ("solo cafe dates", "moving my body") and a small line under it in first person. You can read it in one second.

For youPick your own signature: your colour, font and symbol. Use them on every carousel so it looks like yours, not hers.
The sound

Music on a carousel

It uses "Fill My Cup" by The Dip, a song that matches the caption. Carousels with music can also be shown in the Reels feed, which reaches new people.

For youAlways add a song whose title or lyric matches your headline. Search the theme word in the music picker.
The caption

A little story, then the list

Two honest sentences ("September–December has been an insane grind 😭") before the content. It's seasonal, specific and sounds like a friend talking.

For youMake it Sydney and summer: "November–December is peak season when you do socials for brands…"
Make it a series

One format, many topics

Once the template exists, every theme becomes a new post: how I reset on Sundays, what I eat on a 4-job day, my Sydney solo-date spots.

For youSee ideas 01, 02 and 03 in the idea bank below. One a fortnight gives you a recognisable series.

What's moving right now

Raw beats polished

Reels engagement is up roughly 25% year on year, and phone-shot, unpolished clips are beating studio content. Keep the mistakes in.

Creator community & IRL

Run clubs, creator meet-ups and "come to work with me" content keep rising. It's community content that people tag their friends in.

Your angleA Sydney creator coffee or co-working morning. Each one gives you a collab Reel and new creator friends who share your posts.

02 · How follows happen in 2026

Four rules for reach

Instagram says watch time, likes per reach and sends (shares by DM) are what push a Reel to people who don't follow you. Write every idea so it gets sent.

1

Design for the send

Ask yourself: "Who would they send this to?" Side-hustle chaos, "this is so you" and small-business tips all give people a reason to forward it.

2

Win the first 2 seconds

Put the hook as text on screen in frame one, and start mid-action. Don't open with a logo or intro. Loop the ending back into the start.

3

Test with Trial Reels

Trial Reels show only to non-followers. Post two hooks on the same video, keep the one with better watch time and sends, then share it to your grid.

4

Reels find, carousels keep

Reels bring in new people. Carousels (now with music and a caption per slide) get saved, and that's what turns a visitor into a follower.

06 · Keep watching

The 15-minute trend scan

Trends change weekly, so this page needs topping up. Do this every Sunday before you batch-film, and add anything good to the Radar.

5 min · Instagram

Audio & Reels tab

  • In the Reels editor, open audio and look for the ↗ "trending" arrow. Pick sounds with under 50K Reels; that's early enough.
  • Scroll the Reels tab for 3 minutes. Save anything you'd send to a friend into a "Trend Scan" collection.
  • Open the Edits app's Inspiration tab for templates that are rising.

5 min · TikTok

Where trends start

  • TikTok is usually 1–3 weeks ahead of Instagram. Search #socialmediamanager, #ugccreator, #dayinmylife and #busygirl, and sort by "this week".
  • Check TikTok Creative Center → Trends → Songs & Hashtags, filtered to Australia.
  • Anything big on TikTok but not yet on Reels is your window.

5 min · Your numbers

What worked for you

  • In the Professional dashboard, sort last week's Reels by sends, then by follows.
  • Make a part 2 of the top performer. Drop the format that came last.
  • Read comments for questions. Each one is a Reel idea (reply with a video).
