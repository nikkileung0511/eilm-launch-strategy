Cuts & pacingWhere your cuts actually land

Each tick is a detected cut. Pink ticks open a shot that runs longer than 5 seconds, which in your reels is always a talking moment.

cutopens a long talking shot (>5s)
  • HookFirst cut before 2.0s. Frame one is food or a bite, never an empty room or a black screen.
  • B-roll shots1.0–2.5s each. Cut on the action: the bite, the pull-apart, the sauce drag, the knife.
  • Talking shotsUp to 8s, but punch in to 112% at each new sentence so the frame resets every 3–4s.
  • Length35–50s for a single spot. Up to 70s for a multi-item taste test.
  • TransitionsStraight cuts only. No whips, glitches or 3D wipes; your reels never use them.
  • EndingClose on the verdict line or the shopfront sign, then the Butter Stamp.

Your reels used hot pink in one and plum in another. The sheet makes butter yellow the signature that ties every reel to your name, keeps plum for title cards and keeps pink small, for the ★ only.

TypeThree faces, eight text styles

Your reels used five different faces between them. From now on: Klasyczny (Instagram's Classic) for every caption and aside, Instrument Serif for anything with personality (titles, cards, the location tag) and Hanken Grotesk for labels and reaction pops. Sizes are for a 1080×1920 canvas.

MotionFive moves, and nothing else

  • Punch-inHard cut from 100% to 112% on an emphasis word or a new sentence in a talking shot.
  • Slow pushStatic plate shots drift 100% → 105% over the shot, ease in-out, so nothing sits still.
  • Speed rampFor cheese pulls, sauce drags and pour shots: 100% → 50% → 100%. Film those at 60fps (your Left of Field reel already was).
  • Collage popFor "if you don't know me yet" intros: three past photos land 4 frames apart, scale 92% → 100%, tilted ±4°, hold 1.5s.
  • Type-onOnly for item title cards, 2 frames per character.

Music & soundTwo sound modes

Music-led

For dining diaries without a voiceover, like Aenza (Whisper heard only music). An upbeat track, cuts snapped to the beat, dish labels instead of captions. I'll detect the beats so every cut lands on one.

Voice-led

For bite reviews and taste tests. Your voice is the track. Any music bed sits about 20 dB under your voice and drops out completely for the crunch.

  • The crunchYour "volume up" moment stays: music out, bite sound up 3–4 dB, caption reads < volume up >.
  • LoudnessMaster to −14 LUFS integrated, true peak −1 dB. Your reels already sit here; this locks it in.
  • CleanupLight noise reduction on café voiceovers, high-pass at 80 Hz, no heavy compression.
  • Music choiceYou pick the trending audio in the Instagram app when posting; I'll edit to a placeholder track at the tempo you name, so the cuts still land.
